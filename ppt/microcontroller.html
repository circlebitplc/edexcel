what

A microcontroller is a compact integrated circuit designed to govern a specific operation in an embedded system. A typical microcontroller includes a processor, memory and input/output (I/O) peripherals on a single chip.

why

Microcontroller contains

A microcontroller (MCU for microcontroller unit, or UC for μ-controller) is a small computer on a single integrated circuit. ... A microcontroller contains one or more CPUs (processor cores) along with memory and programmable input/output peripherals.

# SEO Growth Strategy — edexcel.college

**Date:** 11 September 2026  
**Goal:** Qualified organic + AI-search visibility → genuine enquiries and class enrolments  
**Constraint:** Honest positioning (independent tuition college; not Pearson / not an exam centre)

---

## 1. Competitor SEO analysis (legitimate landscape)

| Market | Typical SERP players | What they rank for | Gap for edexcel.college |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sri Lanka | British Council LK (registration), Tutopiya (centre guides), local tuition brands (e.g. A-Star / Excel-style listings) | Private-candidate registration, fee sheets, centre lists, “IGCSE Colombo” | Honest **tuition + process education** for Kandy/Central Province; no fake fee tables |
| Italy / Europe | Online tutors, British Council private-candidate flows | “Edexcel tutor Italy”, home-school exam entry | Country page + online tuition CTA without inventing Milan offices |
| Qatar / Oman / UAE / Gulf | Tutopiya, PapaMarks-style online tutors, British Council Gulf exam pages | Online IGCSE/IAL, centre finder | Country pages that separate **tuition vs centre entry**; Gulf hub already live |
| UK | School centres, Pearson official, UK tutors | Domestic GCSE/A Level vs IAL | Clarify IAL focus for overseas/expat families; avoid UK school-claim spam |
| India | Large online coaching brands | Volume content, subject dumps | Depth on **private candidacy + IAL pathways**, not cloned city doorways |

**Competitor patterns observed**

- Strong pages answer “where do I sit?” (centres) or “who tutors me?” (marketplace tutors).
- Weak pages: thin directory listings, keyword stuffing, implied Pearson affiliation.
- Content opportunities we can win ethically: registration checklists, private-candidate explainers, results/certificates clarity, Kandy campus locality, glossary + FAQ depth, country-specific *tuition* guidance.

**Do not copy** competitor copy, fee lists, or centre databases. Link to Pearson / British Council for authoritative dates and fees.

## 2. SERP opportunity priorities (realistic)

| Opportunity | Why realistic | Primary URLs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FAQ / PAA (registration, private candidate, “are you Pearson?”) | High intent, low competition vs brand giants | `/faq`, guides |
| Long-tail how-to | Featured-snippet friendly answer boxes | `/guides/*` |
| Local — Kandy | Real campus NAP | `/locations/kandy`, `/edexcel-classes` |
| Commercial — classes / exam prep | Enrolment funnel | `/edexcel-classes`, `/exam-preparation` |
| Country + online tuition | Medium competition if unique | `/pearson-exams/{country}` |
| AI overviews | Clear entity + disclaimer + citations | About, hub, glossary |
| Image/video | Later (campus photos, short explainers) | Month 5–6 |

**Deprioritise:** head terms like “Pearson” alone (owned by Pearson); fake multi-city doorways.

---

## 3. Long-tail keyword themes (content = answers)

- How to register for Pearson Edexcel as private candidate  
- Where to take Edexcel exams in Sri Lanka / Qatar / UAE (guide → centre, not fake venue)  
- Edexcel O Level vs IGCSE meaning  
- Statement of entry / what to bring  
- Results and certificate process  
- Online Edexcel tuition Italy / Oman / Maldives  
- Edexcel classes Kandy / Katugastota  

---

## 4. Country + city SEO policy

- **Country pages:** only markets we genuinely support (campus or online/hybrid).  
- **City page:** **Kandy only** (`/locations/kandy`).  
- No Dubai/Doha/Milan office clones.

## 12–13. Quality & AI safeguards

Publish only fact-checked, original, service-consistent pages. No mass-produced country clones.

## 14. Digital PR / backlinks (ethical only)

| Source type | Approach |
| --- | --- |
| Schools / counsellors | Share registration + private-candidate guides |
| Education publications | Offer expert quotes on IGCSE/IAL prep (not Pearson affiliation claims) |
| Local directories | Accurate Kandy NAP (Google Business if claimed) |
| Student communities | Helpful answers + link to glossary/FAQ (no spam) |
| **Never** | PBNs, link farms, automated software, paid spam |

## 15. Brand search

`/about` answers who / what / where / contact / differentiation. Consistent NAP in footer schema.

## 16. Conversion funnel

Search → hub/country/guide → FAQ/trust → Enquire / Contact / Register  
CTAs on all SEO pages; WhatsApp + phone + enquire form.

**Likely drop-offs:** unclear “are you Pearson?”, missing fees for *classes* (office can quote), mobile form friction — monitor after GSC/analytics.
